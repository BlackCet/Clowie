# 🌸 Clowie

> *Your sweet, smart, and delightfully crisp AI scheduling assistant.* ✨

Clowie isn't just another calendar app—she's a conversational AI companion designed to make managing your day feel effortless. Simply tell Clowie what you need to do, and she handles the heavy lifting of scheduling, organizing, and sending you perfectly timed reminders. 

No messy forms. No cluttered interfaces. Just a clean, cute, and intelligent way to plan your life. ☁️

---

## ✨ Features

* **💬 Conversational Scheduling:** Just type "Remind me to call Mom tomorrow at 5 PM" and let Clowie's AI engine automatically categorize, schedule, and save the task.
* **🔐 Seamless Google Auth:** Secure, one-click login using your Google account.
* **💌 Dynamic Email Reminders:** Clowie sends friendly, beautifully formatted reminder emails directly to your inbox when it's time to get things done.
* **☁️ Fully Cloud-Native:** Deployed across a robust, modern cloud infrastructure ensuring high availability and zero downtime.
* **🎨 Crisp & Cute UI:** A minimalist, aesthetic interface that keeps you focused and stress-free.

---

## 🛠️ The Tech Stack

Clowie is built with a powerful, modern full-stack architecture:

**Frontend (The Face) 🎀**
* **React & Vite:** Lightning-fast UI rendering.
* **Tailwind CSS & Lucide Icons:** For that crisp, clean, and beautiful aesthetic.
* **Hosting:** Render static site

**Backend (The Brain) 🧠**
* **Java & Spring Boot:** A robust, enterprise-grade server engine.
* **Gemini AI API:** Powers Clowie's natural language understanding and schedule extraction.
* **Spring Mail:** Handles automated, dynamic email dispatching.
* **Hosting:** Dockerized and deployed on Render.

**Database (The Memory) 📚**
* **PostgreSQL:** Reliable relational data storage.
* **Neon.tech:** Serverless, cloud-hosted database.
